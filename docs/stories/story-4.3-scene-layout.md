# Story 4.3: Scene layout: geometry, places, memory and words

**Status:** Approved

## Story

As the author who learns where each project lives in the workshop,
I want one pure function that turns the board, relay and registry payloads into places, poses, targets and words, with slots remembered on this machine,
so that the scene is the same every time for the same payloads, nothing moves unless a state changed, and every rule is tested without a browser.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Geometry (AD-61, D-104): `app/src/lib/scene/geometry.ts` holds the constants and the projection ported from the prototype's `layout.mjs` (`GEOM`, `P`, the cells, `DESKS`, the antechamber seats, the plaque cap and anchor), the canvas computed from them as the prototype does, `TARGET_CSS_MIN` (44), `MIN_SCALE` as `TARGET_CSS_MIN / GEOM.TARGET.w`, `MAX_SCALE`, `TARGET_MIN` (`ceil(TARGET_CSS_MIN / MIN_SCALE)`), and one function or constant per target rectangle of D-104: figure, "+n" chip, register overflow, More workshops, shelf, cabinet, lamp, both dials. Tests import them, never retype them (AD-60). Proof: the cases of AC 2 and the layout cases below, which use only these exports; Vitest `app/src/tokens.test.ts > TARGET_CSS_MIN equals app.css's --target-min` (review F23).
2. Geometry property (AD-70, D-104): built from `geometry.ts` alone, at full occupancy in two variants (eight workshops with four desks and a chip each; seven such workshops and More workshops in slot 8), each with nine seats, the register's overflow, lamp, both dials, shelf and cabinet: no two targets intersect; no two plaques at their 140 px cap intersect; no target meets a plaque at its cap other than the one it owns; every target side is `TARGET_MIN` or more, so 44 CSS px at `MIN_SCALE`; every target lies inside the canvas. Proof: Vitest `app/src/lib/scene/geometry.test.ts > no two targets intersect at full occupancy`, `> no two plaques intersect at their cap` (review F25), `> no target meets a plaque it does not own`, `> every target is 44 CSS px or more at the minimum scale`, `> every target lies inside the canvas`.
3. The function (AD-60, AD-68): `app/src/lib/scene/layout.ts` exports `layoutScene(input: SceneInput, memory: SceneMemory): SceneLayout`, with `SceneInput = { board: Board; relay: RelayView | null; registry: RegistryView | null }`, and no DOM, clock, storage, flag or `Math.random`. `SceneLayout` holds: rooms (the antechamber; workshops by slot with project, `dimmed`, plaque text and count, four desks each empty, occupied or kept by a waiting session, and the chip's list; More workshops with its projects and sessions); employees (card key, place, pose, logical point, number 1 to 9 or `null`, and `enteredAt` for antechamber occupants, for the halo); the shelf (source `registry` or `board`, items with name and project); the cabinet (source, count, label); the register (rows of the first nine, overflow list); targets in tab order (id, group, kind, rectangle, `aria-label`, name tag, the card key or the keys of the group's list); the text alternative; and the next `SceneMemory`. Lamp and dials join in story 4.7. Proof: Vitest `app/src/lib/scene/layout.test.ts > identical input gives a deep-equal layout`, `> the layout is a fixed point of its own memory` (D-102), each over every board of the fixture index (D-181).
4. Slots (FR-1 as amended, AD-62, D-99, D-102): floor projects first in `floor_projects` order, a repeated name keeping its first place only; then remembered projects with a live card on the first layout; then projects in order of first appearance, ties by `started_at_ms` (`null` last), project name, card key; a slot is kept until restart and dimmed without a live session; with more than eight projects slot 8 is More workshops, whose plaque counts the extra projects; "other" and "unknown" are ordinary projects; To resume and Done cards take no slot. Proof: `layout.test.ts > floor projects take the first slots in file order`, `> a repeated floor project name takes no second slot` (review F33), `> remembered projects with a live card come next`, `> new projects follow in order of first appearance`, `> ties go to the earliest start, then the name`, `> a project keeps its slot and dims when its sessions end`, `> a ninth project turns slot 8 into More workshops`, `> ended and done sessions take no slot`, `> other and unknown are ordinary projects`.
5. Desks (AD-62, D-102, D-107): four per workshop; a live session keeps its desk while live, waiting included, and that desk shows its number when it is one of the first nine; a new session takes the lowest free desk in the tie order; a live session without a free desk goes to the chip's list and stays there until its own column changes, even when a desk frees up, then takes the lowest free desk if any (review F10); a desk entry lives while its key is a live card of that project. Proof: `layout.test.ts > a session keeps its desk while it waits`, `> a waiting session's desk shows its number up to nine`, `> an ended session frees its desk`, `> a new session takes the lowest free desk`, `> a fifth live session goes to the chip's list`, `> a session in the chip's list keeps its place when a desk frees up`, `> a session in the chip's list takes a free desk when its own column changes`.
6. Antechamber and poses (FR-2 as amended, AD-61, D-102, D-103, D-107): Needs you cards sit in board order on seats 0 to 8, rows of three, front row first, numbered 1 to 9; from the tenth on they go to the register's list; the register names the first nine in board order; a Your turn card has the Your turn pose, a Working card with the `unknown` badge the Unknown pose, any other Working card the Working pose. Only state changes move (FR-3 as amended, D-102): changing one card's column moves that card and the antechamber occupants whose board index changed, nothing else; the expected movers are computed from the two boards, never typed. Proof: `layout.test.ts > waiting sessions sit in board order numbered 1 to 9`, `> the register names the first nine waiting in board order` (review F25), `> the tenth waiting session goes to the register's list`, `> the pose follows column and badge` (iterating the exhaustive records of `text.ts`), `> only the changed card and the antechamber occupants whose board index changed move` (for every live card of every fixture board, each changed board built in core's order) (review F10).
7. Shelf and cabinet (FR-1 as amended, AD-68 table, D-112): with a readable registry, the shelf holds the subjects with status `to_resume` and the cabinet counts `archived_count`; without one, or with one not readable, the shelf holds the board's To resume cards and the cabinet counts Done, labelled from `done_window_ms`. Proof: `layout.test.ts > without a registry the shelf holds To resume and the cabinet counts Done`, `> with a registry the shelf holds subjects to resume and the cabinet the archived count`, `> an unreadable registry counts as absent`.
8. Targets and arrows (AD-63, D-103, D-104, D-187): tab order is the antechamber's figures in board order, the register's overflow, workshops by slot with desks front to back and each chip after its desks, More workshops, shelf, cabinet; groups are the antechamber (its figures and the register's overflow, review F25), each workshop (its desks and chip), More workshops, and the walls; `nearestTarget(targets, fromId, direction)` returns, within the focused target's group, the nearest target whose centre lies within 45 degrees of the arrow's axis (perpendicular offset not larger than the forward offset, forward offset above 0), ties by tab order, `null` when none. Proof: `layout.test.ts > targets follow AD-63's tab order`, `> arrows follow the vectors of a full workshop and a full antechamber` (every arrow from every desk and every seat, expected target ids as test data), `> an arrow with no target within 45 degrees goes nowhere` (review F13).
9. Words (AD-63, D-107): `app/src/lib/scene/text.ts` builds the text alternative and every `aria-label` and name tag from the layout: counts and names, never a relative time; state words from exhaustive `Record<Column, string>` and `Record<Badge, string>`. Proof: Vitest `app/src/lib/scene/text.test.ts > the summary names the waiting count and the oldest waiting session`, `> nobody waiting reads as such`, `> every figure's name states session, project, state and its number up to nine`, `> group targets name their count`, `> every column and badge has a word`.
10. Memory (AD-62, D-102, D-184): `app/src/lib/scene/memory.ts` keeps the current `SceneMemory` in a plain module variable, not reactive state; `load(firstBoard)` reads `localStorage["officina.scene.v1"]` (`{ v: 1, floor, slots, desks }`) once, discards it when `board.floor` differs, and prunes it against that board before the first layout; `commit(next)` replaces the variable and writes storage only when the assignment changed; an unreadable or foreign value starts empty; a storage that throws costs only stability. Proof: Vitest `app/src/lib/scene/memory.test.ts > load prunes projects and desks without a live card on the first board`, `> load discards a memory of another floor`, `> commit writes only when the assignment changes`, `> an unreadable or foreign value starts empty`, `> a storage that throws keeps the layout working` (review F6).
11. Fixtures before Vitest (D-101, D-181): the CI `windows` job runs "e2e fixtures from the real CLI" before the step running `pnpm test`; the layout tests read boards through `app/e2e/fixture-index.ts`, which fails, naming `pnpm e2e:fixtures`, when the index is missing. Proof: the CI `windows` job.
12. Documentation (D-121): the README's building-from-source tests block runs `pnpm e2e:fixtures` before `pnpm test` (review F36). Proof: review.

## Dev Notes

- Requirements: FR-1, FR-2, FR-3 (only state changes move), FR-6 (tab order, sizes, words) as amended. Decisions: AD-60, AD-61, AD-62, AD-63, AD-68, AD-70 (layout unit, geometry property). Epic decisions: P-19, P-20, D-98, D-99, D-101 to D-104, D-107, D-112, D-181, D-184, D-187.
- Start from the prototype (`epic-4-decisions.md` says where): `layout.mjs` is the shape of this function and its geometry; `render.mjs` shows where each element and target sits; `search.mjs` and the collision check of `mock.mjs` are the ancestors of the property test. The prototype reads floor projects from a side file; here they come from `board.floor_projects` (story 4.1). The prototype's `others` room, chip and register overflow are incomplete, and its desk rule lets a chip-list session take a freed desk: follow D-102, D-103 and D-104, not the prototype, for them.
- The owner's review of `scene-mock.png` (R27) is recorded in this story's Completion Notes when it is available; it is not an entry condition (review F17). Every geometry constant lives in `geometry.ts`, so a change after that review is a constant edit plus a re-run of the property test, never a change of the property.
- D-104's starting values were checked on the prototype's geometry; if the port changes a constant and the property fails, fix the geometry, never the property.
- AD-13 holds: the layout reads `card.column` and `card.badge` and maps them; it never classifies. A Working card with the `unknown` badge is the board's "cannot tell" (`board.rs` places unknown in Working). Needs you is sorted by activity, oldest first (`board.rs`), so a new wait can enter ahead of others: AC 6's movers come from the boards, not from a rule of thumb (D-102).
- Seams (D-98): `RelayView` (2.6) and `RegistryView` (3.4) are imported as types; if 3.4 has not merged, AC 7's registry cases wait and `registry` is typed `null` until then. The registry state that counts as readable is story 3.4's.
- The fixed point (D-102) and the plain memory variable (D-184) work together: story 4.4 commits the returned memory from an effect, which must not re-run the layout, and the next payload must give the same places.
- Relative times are never drawn (AD-64): `text.ts` imports nothing from `lib/time.ts`.
- `app/src/tokens.test.ts` starts here with the `--target-min` case; story 4.4 adds the contrast cases.
