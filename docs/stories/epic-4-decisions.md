# Epic 4 decisions

D-124 to D-179 belong to Epic 5.

What the PRD, the addendum, the mock and the code leave open or state twice for v0.4, settled while writing the Epic 4 stories on 2026-10-28 under the owner's delegation of design decisions, then reworked the same day after a review of the stories: D-180 to D-189 are new, and a decision edited in place names the finding (`review Fn`) that changed it. Numbering continues Epic 3's (`epic-3-decisions.md`), so a `P-n` or `D-n` is unique across epics and Epic 4 stories cite all three files. Stories cite these decisions and name the test that proves each one; this file states the rule, never the test data. A later change edits this file with the fact that motivates it, like the architecture.

The scene was reworked again on 2026-10-28 after the owner approved the interactive prototype v2: section "Scene v2 rework" below (D-460 to D-485) comes first and wins over the earlier sections where they differ; D-461 lists what it retires and amends.

"The prototype" below is `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/design/prototype/`, git-ignored with the mock: a working sketch of AD-60 to port from, never imported, absent from CI. "The prototype v2" is its `interactive/` folder.

- **D-461.** Retired: D-100 (archived count), D-112 (shelf and cabinet), D-115, D-116 (instruments), D-183 (quota reset flags), D-186 (`seatMove`). Amended, read with "antechamber" as "the decision office's queue", "workshop" as "office" and "More workshops" as "More offices": D-101 (scenarios: D-464's approvals and D-466's priorities join; scene waits stay at least 3 minutes old, under the default threshold only when the scenario does not test alerts), D-102 (desks taken on the first layout outside reception), D-103 (groups, widened by D-484), D-104 (target sizes: figure 48, the property; the lamp, dial, shelf, cabinet values go), D-107 (numbers on the queue and the alert room), D-109 (motion durations: AD-172's constants, zero under reduced motion), D-110 (the lint exception of D-470), D-117 (the prototype v2's tokens join), D-119 (baselines compared with the prototype v2's renders), D-120 (two more runs, AD-71 as amended), P-20 (its list of departures loses the retired decisions), P-22 (the bump covers `floor_projects`, `last_message_tail`, `ProjectView.priority`, `Card.scene`, the three alert fields and, if gate 1 passes, `Card.subagents`; no registry view bump). Every other decision holds. (all)
- **D-462.** The scene places the board's sessions; it never reads raw signals (AD-13, AD-167). The prototype queued an `idle_prompt` notification at the decision office; Claude Code sends it when a session has sat idle at its prompt, which the board already shows as Your turn, and `board.rs`'s `BLOCKING_NOTIFICATIONS` does not count it. So `idle_prompt` moves nobody, Your turn is the lounge, and only Needs you and pending approvals queue. A question asked through a dialog is Needs you already (`elicitation_dialog`, or the agents status). (4.3)
- **D-463.** `PreToolUse` is not a scene signal in v0.4. The hook records five events (`doctor.rs` `BUFFERED_EVENTS`), and `PreToolUse` reaches it only for gated tools in guarded projects, where the gate answers and records nothing (D-18, D-171). Registering it for every tool would cost a process start per tool call (E9). Working comes from `claude agents` (`busy`), and a working screen is lit steadily; the prototype's per-tool screen flicker is dropped. (4.3, 4.6)
- **D-464.** A session whose relay approval is pending (`board.approvals`, `state` pending, matched by `session_id`) queues at the decision office after the numbered Needs you sessions, in `requested_at_ms` order, with an amber approval ticket without a digit: approvals have no key (D-28, D-107). Its file shows the approval and a button to Board mode, where it is approved on its card, pointer only. An approval with no matching live card is not drawn; the header pill counts it. (4.3, 4.5, 4.7)
- **D-465.** Alert (FR-10, AD-167): a waiting session is in alert when its project's priority is high, or when `waiting_since_ms` is known and the wait reached the threshold. `waiting_since_ms` is the latest blocking notification after the last prompt and the last stop, or the earliest pending approval's request time; an agents `waiting` status with no such notification has no known start and never escalates by time, and the file says "since unknown". An alerted session keeps its board number and leaves the alert room when it stops waiting. Your turn never alerts: the lounge is the non-urgent place. (4.3, 4.8)
- **D-466.** Floor keys (AD-168): `[[project]] priority = "normal" | "high"`, default normal, and `[alert] after_minutes`, 1 to 1440, default 10. They authorise nothing, so they stay out of the relay's canonical catalogue and its approval, and apply at the next floor load; doctor reports a bad value with its line and the default stays in force. `ProjectColor` gains `plum` (the prototype v2's fourth colour). (4.2)
- **D-467.** Escalation by time is computed in core at `now_ms`; the board carries `next_escalation_at_ms`, and the collector recomputes from its cached inputs, without spawning `claude`, when its 250 ms tick reaches it (AD-168). This keeps the scene clock-free and the alert within a tick of its threshold. (4.3)
- **D-468.** Offices (AD-169): four in the base floor; the fifth and sixth projects add a column of two, the seventh and eighth another; a ninth turns office 8 into More offices at once (D-102's rule for slot 8). The floor grows only; columns stay until restart, like a dimmed slot. Office slots follow AD-62's order. (4.5)
- **D-469.** Walking is the default (`ViewState.walking`, default `true`, D-113's field-by-field rule), with a "Walking" toggle in the header in Scene mode. Reduced motion is the system's, read at each move, never stored: figures are placed at once. The first layout of a run places everyone at once; only later changes walk. (4.6)
- **D-470.** The one frame loop of the scene is `lib/scene/mover.ts`'s, started by a changed place or a fade and stopped when nothing moves (AD-64 as amended, AD-172). D-110's lint lists, in `src/lib/scene/**` and `src/components/scene/**`, `setInterval`, `setTimeout`, `requestAnimationFrame`, `Date.now`, `performance.now` and `Math.random`, with one override exempting `src/lib/scene/mover.ts` from the first two time sources and `requestAnimationFrame`; the lint test proves the exemption covers that file only. The mover writes `data-moving` on the scene element so the e2e knows when the scene has settled. (4.6)
- **D-471.** A figure's target sits at the place the layout gives it (AD-63 as amended): when the state changes, the target moves at once and the figure walks there; a walking figure is not clickable on its way (`pointer-events: none` everywhere but layer 5). Gives up: clicking a figure mid-walk, which lasts a few seconds. Targets keep AD-70's property at 44 CSS px; the prototype v2's queue pitch (1.1 tile, 44 logical px apart on the diagonal) and lounge seats (0.7 tile) are too close for disjoint 48 px targets, so the port spreads or removes spots until the property passes, and never weakens it. (4.5)
- **D-472.** Instruments stay in the header, not in the scene (AD-177): the quota gauges (v0.1) and the relay chip and approvals pill (v0.2) are visible in every mode, and the prototype v2 dropped the scene's instruments to keep the eye on movement. - **D-473.** The alert band (AD-175) sits under the header, above every other banner, in every view and mode; it names the first alerted session in board order, its project, its waiting reason and its cause, and counts the others; it has no dismiss and leaves with the last alert. - **D-474.** The beacon's glow breathes under AD-65's memo keyed on the first alerted card's `(key, waiting_since_ms)`, two minutes, then steady, and steady under reduced motion; the prototype v2's rotating beam is drawn static. Both keep FR-9: no animation runs for as long as an alert lasts. (4.8)

## Layout

- **D-102.** A "card" in AD-62 is a live card (Needs you, Your turn, Working): To resume and Done cards never take a slot or a desk; they are the shelf and the cabinet. Ties within one payload, for slots and for desks: `started_at_ms` ascending with `null` last, then project name, then card key. "Start" is the first layout of the window's run, whatever mode was shown before: `memory.ts` prunes the stored memory against that layout's board (D-184). A desk entry is kept while its key is a live card of that project. A ninth project turns slot 8 into More workshops at once, and slot 8's project joins it (AD-62). The layout is a fixed point of its own memory: laying out the same input again with the memory it returned gives a deep-equal layout.

  Two moves follow from FR-2 and AD-62 and are settled here (review F10). A live session without a desk stays in its workshop's chip list until its own column changes, even when a desk frees up, and only then takes the lowest free desk if one is free: FR-3 stays literal, and a desk can stand empty beside a "+n" chip for a while. In the antechamber, beside AD-62's freed seat, a seat ahead taken by a session the board ranks earlier (Needs you is sorted by activity, oldest first, so a wait whose activity is older enters ahead) also moves the occupants behind it: FR-2's board order wins. The "only state changes move" test computes the expected movers: the changed card plus every antechamber occupant whose board index changed. (4.3)
- **D-103.** Three groups can hold more sessions than their place: the waiting beyond nine (the register), a workshop's live sessions beyond its four desks (the "+n" chip) and the projects beyond the seventh slot (More workshops). Escape from a listed file returns to the list, Escape from the list to the group's target. Drawn: the register's tenth row reads "+N more", the chip "+n", and More workshops names its sessions on its floor while rows fit, then "+m more". This is how FR-2's and AD-62's "each focusable and actionable" holds. It departs from AD-63's register rows beyond nine as tab stops and AD-62's "one target each" in More workshops, and from amended FR-2's "it names the others" (P-20): on the prototype's geometry the nine register rows end at about y 185 logical px while the wall top under the register's right edge is at about y 203, and a cell holds two or three 48 px rows, so one 44 px target per name does not fit. Tab order and groups: the register's target belongs to the antechamber's group, right after its figures (review F25); a chip follows its workshop's desks, in that workshop's group; More workshops follows the last workshop. (4.3, 4.5)
- **D-104.** Every scene target is at least `ceil(44 / MIN_SCALE)` logical px on each side, so 44 CSS px at the minimum scale, and AD-70's geometry property asserts it for every target, not only figures: AD-61's 44 x 44 chip and 44 x 46 lamp give 40 CSS px at the minimum scale, and the prototype's archive target is 32 px wide. (4.3, 4.7)

(4.3)

## Motion and halo

- **D-108.** `lib/halo.ts` keeps, per `(card key, enteredAt)`, `{ startMs, iterations }`: `startMs` is the first-sight time rounded down to a multiple of the 4 s cycle, and `iterations` the smallest odd count of 2 s halves with `startMs + iterations x 2000 >= enteredAt + HALO_BREATHING_MS`, or 0 when the wait is already older than `HALO_BREATHING_MS` at first sight. Each halo element, in the scene's layer 2 and on the board card, takes `animation-delay: startMs - mountMs` once, when it mounts, through a Svelte action of `lib/halo.ts`; nothing rewrites it while it stays mounted, and the action re-applies only when the pair changes (Svelte calls `update` on every new parameter object). This refines AD-65's `-(firstSeenMs mod 4000)`, which keeps the shared phase and the two-minute end only at first sight: a remount (Board to Scene and back, Focus off) restarted the breathing, out of phase. `enteredAt` is `last_event_at_ms`, else `started_at_ms`, else the first-sight time. One instance, `haloMemo`, exported by `lib/halo.ts`, serves the action and `BoardStore`, which drops the keys no longer in Needs you when a board arrives; it is a plain `Map`, not reactive state, so pruning never re-renders, and the action reads `mountMs` from the memo's injected clock (review F19). The resting opacity is one custom property, `--halo-rest`, used by the keyframes' `to` and by the halo's base style (review F23). `HALO_BREATHING_MS` is the one constant FR-9's fallback sets to 0. (4.2)
