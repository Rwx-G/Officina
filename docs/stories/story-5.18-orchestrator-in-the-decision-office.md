# Story 5.18: The orchestrator in the decision office, the briefing walk and the view switch's mark

**Status:** In progress (ACs 2 to 9 and 11 done; AC 1 and AC 10's baselines are the owner's steps; the entry also waits on 5.16 AC 10, gate 1's window half, D-841)

## Story

As the author who looks at the workshop all day,
I want the foreman drawn at the decider's desk of v0.4's decision office, with an empty chair and Start when it is absent, its screens and whiteboard showing what it proposed, a short walk to the reception counter when a work order it can brief arrives, and a quiet mark on the Orchestrator view switch,
so that I see at a glance whether the orchestrator is there and what it suggested, without anything it does ever looking like something waiting on me, and without the scene losing an office or scrolling on my screen (PRD section 9 items 1 and 2).
